Setting up

  1. Open Friction and tap Set up protection.
  2. Tap Allow Screen Time access and approve Apple's prompt.
  3. Tap Choose Instagram. In Apple's picker, expand Social and select only Instagram, not the whole category.
  4. Tap Turn on protection. Optionally allow notifications.

Unlocking Instagram

When Instagram is blocked, open Friction (or tap Start a challenge on the block screen, then the "Your challenge is ready" notification). Complete the short typing challenge, choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, and tap Open Instagram. When the time is up, Instagram is blocked again. Tap Lock now to end a session early.

Each unlock on the same day makes the next challenge a little longer. Progress resets every day at midnight.

Common questions

The block screen doesn't appear. Open Friction, tap Protection, and check that Screen Time access is allowed and Instagram is selected. Screen Time access can be removed in iOS Settings.

"Start a challenge" just closes Instagram. Notifications for Friction are off. Turn them on in Settings, or open Friction from your Home Screen and tap Start challenge.

How do I stop using Friction? Open Protection and tap Turn off protection, or delete the app.
