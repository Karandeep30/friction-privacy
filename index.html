Friction Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to Friction (listed on the App Store as Social Breaker: Scroll Pause) for iPhone.

Friction adds a short pause before you open Instagram. It is built to work entirely on your iPhone. Friction does not collect, sell, share or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Friction saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your device:

  • Today's counters: how many challenges you started, completed and missed, and how many minutes you unlocked. These reset each day.
  • Your current unlocked session: when it started, when it ends, the length you chose, and the optional reason you picked (for example "Reply to someone").
  • Your preferred session length and whether protection is turned on.
  • The app you chose to block, stored as an opaque token from Apple's Screen Time. Apple keeps app names private, so Friction cannot see which app the token refers to.

This information never leaves your device through Friction. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, as with any app's data. Deleting Friction removes it.

Screen Time

Friction uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to block the app you select and to show the block screen. Friction can only apply or remove that block; it cannot see your activity, usage history or the contents of any app. Screen Time access is granted, and can be removed at any time, in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Friction shows a local "Your challenge is ready" notification when you tap Start a challenge on the block screen. Notifications are created on your device; Friction does not use push notifications or a server.

Shortcuts and Siri

Friction offers Shortcuts actions that check whether Instagram needs unlocking and open a challenge. They read only the on-device information described above.

Opening Instagram

When you choose to open Instagram, Friction asks iOS to open the Instagram app. Friction sends Instagram no information. Your use of Instagram is covered by Instagram's own privacy policy.

Children

Friction does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data at all.

Changes

If Friction's handling of information ever changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
